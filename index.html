Why might an available name still not work?+

We show “Available” when no public profile is found. This isn't a guarantee: Twitch may still reserve the name after a rename, suspension or deactivation, and it doesn't offer a precise public check for those cases. Look at the confirmation field and check the name on Twitch before choosing it.

How do I confirm I can use a name?+

Sign in to Twitch, then open Settings → Profile and edit your username. Try the name there before choosing it. Some names are held back even without a visible profile. Read Twitch's username policy ↗. Availability can change before you save the new username.

Do I need to sign in here?+

No login or password needed. We send the username to Twitch to check it. Searches are not saved.
