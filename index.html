    “Available” means confirmed by Twitch. “Possibly available” is an unconfirmed result.

    What's the difference between available and possibly available?+

    “Available” means Twitch confirmed you can use the name at the time of the check. “Possibly available” means we found no public profile, but Twitch didn't confirm availability. A name can still be reserved after a rename, suspension or deactivation. The warning is part of the result, so check on Twitch before choosing it.

    How do I confirm I can use a name?+

    Try the name when signing up for Twitch, or in Settings → Profile if you already have an account. Some names are held back even without a visible profile. Read Twitch's username policy ↗. A name can be taken between checking and signing up.

    Do I need to sign in here?+

    No login or password needed. We send the username to Twitch to check it. Your recent searches stay in this browser; use Clear history to remove them.
